Chicken Haleem

Chicken Haleem is a hearty, flavorful stew made with slow-cooked chicken, lentils, and cracked wheat. This traditional South Asian dish is rich in protein and bursting with aromatic spices. Perfect for special occasions or when you want a comforting meal!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cracked wheat (dalia)
  • 1/4 cup chana dal (split chickpeas)
  • 1/4 cup masoor dal (red lentils)
  • 1/4 cup moong dal (yellow lentils)
  • 500g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 onions, finely sliced
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2-3 green chilies, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons coriander powder
  • 1 tablespoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 6-7 cups water

For Garnish

  • Fried onions
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Green chilies, sliced
  • Ginger, julienned
  • Lemon wedges
  • Mint leaves (optional)

Method

  1. Wash and soak the cracked wheat and all lentils together for 1 hour, then drain.
  2. In a large pot, add chicken pieces, soaked wheat and lentils, chopped onions, ginger-garlic paste, and all spices.
  3. Add water and bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for about 1.5-2 hours until meat is very tender.
  4. Remove chicken pieces, shred the meat, and return it to the pot.
  5. Using a wooden spoon or hand blender, mash the mixture to create a paste-like consistency.
  6. Heat oil or ghee in a separate pan and add to the Haleem.
  7. Simmer for another 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.
  8. The Haleem should have a thick, porridge-like consistency. Add more hot water if it becomes too thick.
  9. Serve hot, garnished with fried onions, fresh coriander, green chilies, ginger, and a squeeze of lemon.

Serve Chicken Haleem hot with naan bread or rice. It tastes even better the next day as the flavors develop more!
