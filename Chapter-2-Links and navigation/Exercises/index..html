Overview

3 Idiots is a 2009 Indian coming-of-age comedy-drama film directed by Rajkumar Hirani and produced by Vidhu Vinod Chopra. The film is loosely adapted from Chetan Bhagat's novel "Five Point Someone" and follows the friendship of three students at an Indian engineering college.

The story focuses on the pressure of the Indian education system and how it affects students' lives. Through humor and heart-touching moments, the film challenges the conventional notions of success and encourages viewers to pursue excellence in their passion rather than chase success for its own sake.

Characters

Rancho

A gifted student who believes in understanding concepts rather than rote learning. He challenges the educational system and inspires his friends to pursue their passions.

Farhan

An engineering student whose true passion is wildlife photography. He struggles between following his dreams and meeting his father's expectations.

Raju

A student from a poor family under immense pressure to succeed and support his family. His fear of failure often paralyzes him.

Virus

The strict college director who believes in rigid discipline and traditional education methods. He is Pia's father and Rancho's nemesis.
